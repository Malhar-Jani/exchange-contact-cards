# Exchange design

Operate mode: contact editor and live QR preview. The contact is the artifact, with a clear separation between private event organisation and public contact data.

Warm paper ground `#f7f6f2`, ink `#252923`, muted text `#697064`, divider `#dedfd6`, orange action `#c43d1d`, pale olive contact card `#e9eddf`. Self-hosted DM Sans at 400–700. Headings use restrained negative tracking. White QR field and four-module quiet zone remain unmodified for scanner reliability.

Desktop uses a wider form and a sticky preview divided by a hairline. Mobile stacks editor and QR with full-width primary action. Standard form labels, visible focus, native confirmation for discarding edits/deletion, and native dialog for protected presentation mode. 44px minimum action heights. Motion is limited to hover feedback and disabled under reduced-motion preference.
