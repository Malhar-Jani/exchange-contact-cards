# Exchange
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->
## Platform
web
## Stack
Delegated by user: static React, TypeScript, Vite, compatible with GitHub Pages and a personal subdomain.
## Users
People sharing contacts at events using phones.
## Product Purpose
Generate contact QR codes with configurable details for each event. Recipients scan and confirm saving through their phones.
## Capabilities and Constraints
Current scope: one-way vCard QR generation, locally saved event presets, large QR display, QR PNG and vCard downloads. QR contains contact data directly for offline sharing. No recipient registration. No automatic address-book writes or reciprocal exchange. Scanner support varies. No backend, accounts, tracking, or device sync. No actual personal contact details supplied; placeholders are illustrative.
## Product Principles
Minimum setup. Explicit sharing. Honest local persistence. Optional fields stay optional. Event labels are private; contact notes are shared.
